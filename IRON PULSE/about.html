About Us

Built By Lifters,
For Lifters

Iron Pulse started in a single rented room with ten dumbbells. Nine years later, it's a full training floor and a coaching team that still remembers everyone's name.

Our Story

From One Room To A Full Floor

We opened in 2017 with a simple idea: strength training shouldn't be intimidating, and it shouldn't be watered down either. What began as a small strength club has grown into a full gym with a dedicated cardio zone, a group class studio, and a coaching bench of eighteen certified trainers.

We still run on the same principle we started with — every member gets a program that fits their goals, not a generic template pulled off a shelf.
